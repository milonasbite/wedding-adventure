💍 Wedding Challenge

Ready to play?

Rotate your phone to landscape if you can. Push the joystick up for a single jump. Use JUMP ×2 and tap it again while airborne for a double jump. Collect hearts to survive hits and falls. If your heart count is 0, the next hit or fall ends the run. Watch out for the Frankenstein-style monsters, mischievous cats, and seagulls patrolling the upper lanes. You also get 1 flamethrower burst at a time, and a fuel pickup can refill it once. There are 4 levels. Find the key in Level 4 before you reach the bride's cage.
